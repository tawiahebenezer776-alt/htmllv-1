ABOUT LITTLE STARS DAYCARE

little stars Daycare is a safe,caring and fun evironment where children can learn ,play, and grow. Our website helps parents learn about our program, activites enrollment , and children services while staying connected with their children daily experiences.

Funactions/service of little stars daycare

• Childcare: Provides safe and caring supervision for children.
• Learning: Teaches children basic academic and social skills through age-appropriate activities.
• Activities: Provides games, drawing, music, storytelling, and creative activities.
• Feeding: Provides meals and snacks for children.
• Rest: Provides a comfortable place for children to nap and rest.
• Personal care: Helps children with hygiene and daily routines.
• Parent communication: Keeps parents updated about their child's activities and progress.
• Safety: Maintains a secure environment for children.
